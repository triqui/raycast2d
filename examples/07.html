07 - Definitions and filters

new TileGrid({ map, tileSize, definitions })
A definition is whatever you want it to be. The library reads none of its fields: it hands the definition of whatever the ray met to the filter, on candidate.userData, and lets it decide.

  • wall: stops sight, bodies and shots.
  • grate: stops bodies and shots, but can be seen through.
  • glass: stops bodies only.
  • water: stops nothing.

options.filter
A filter answers one question: does this stop the ray? That is where the rules of the problem live, and why the library ships almost none of them.
The three colored rays are three casts along the same line, over the same map, with three different one-line filters, and they stop in three different places.

lineOfSight(startX, startY, endX, endY, options)
The same cast reduced to a yes or no, for when the contact does not matter and only the answer does.
