02 - Every contact along a ray

Drag the ring to reposition the source. The cross marks the destination and follows the pointer.

castAll(originX, originY, directionX, directionY, maxDistance, options)
Casts a ray and returns every object or tile along it as an array of RayHit, ordered from the nearest.
The array is empty if the ray reaches its full length without hitting anything.

  • It takes a direction and a length rather than a second point, so the two ends are turned into a delta first.
  • options.limit keeps only the first few contacts.

A wall has two surfaces, and a cast only reports the one facing the ray: an exit is a surface met from behind.
So the ray is cast twice, the second time from the far end backwards, which turns every exit into an entry the library reports normally.

Pairing the two lists also tells real surfaces from false ones. castAll answers once per solid cell, so the boundary between the two halves of a thick wall is reported as well, and being inside the material it comes back from both directions. A real surface comes back from one direction only.

each(originX, originY, directionX, directionY, maxDistance, callback, options)
Walks along the ray and calls the callback once for every cell it reaches, including empty cells.
